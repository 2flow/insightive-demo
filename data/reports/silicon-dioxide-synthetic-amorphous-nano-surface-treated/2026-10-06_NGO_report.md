## 1. Summary

There are no top signals: none target the biocide approval, and none target any other regime for this substance. No NGO page in the window names the PT18 active substance (pyrogenic, surface-treated nano silica, CAS 68909-20-6) or its insecticide use. NGO attention to nano silica is limited to broader issues. French consumer group UFC-Que Choisir rates the food additive E551 (amorphous silica, partly in nano form) as "not very recommendable". Inf'OGM's 2025 piece on agricultural nanomaterials mentions silica nanoparticles only in passing. Both items concern related, non-surface-treated silica or nanomaterials as a class. The NGO Avicenn/VeilleNanos keeps a dossier on silica nanoparticles, but its pages could not be opened. Overall, NGOs are watching nano silica in food and cosmetics in a general way, with nothing pointing at the biocide file.

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

No NGO has announced a campaign, case or deadline on this substance. Context only (not an NGO finding): the European Commission asked the SCCS in October 2025 for a fresh opinion on nano silica, silica silylate and silica dimethyl silylate in cosmetics. Silica silylate and silica dimethyl silylate are close relatives of the surface-treated form. This could draw NGO comment (e.g. from Avicenn or consumer groups) in the coming months and is worth watching before the 2028 BPR expiry.
