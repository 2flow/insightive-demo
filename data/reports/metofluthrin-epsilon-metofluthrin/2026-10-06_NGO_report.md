0 of 0 top signals: no NGO item in the window names metofluthrin or epsilon-metofluthrin, so none target the biocide approval directly or indirectly. Across the named European NGOs (PAN Europe, PAN Germany, PAN UK, Générations Futures, GLOBAL 2000, ClientEarth, BeeLife, HEAL, BUND, Greenpeace, WildFish, Foodwatch, CEO, Friends of the Earth) and the US group Beyond Pesticides, I found no campaign, letter, study, consultation response or litigation on this substance. The only related pressure is class-level work on pyrethroids. Générations Futures (2025-04-25) called for all pyrethroid-based products, biocides included, to be withdrawn from the market. PAN Germany (2025-06 and 2025-11) wrote about how pyrethroid insect sprays are sold and about how toxic pyrethroids are to aquatic life. Neither names metofluthrin. Overall direction: there is general NGO hostility to pyrethroids as a class, but none of it is aimed at metofluthrin, and nothing suggests a campaign is building against the PT18 approval (renewed in 2025) or the pending PT19 decision.

## 3. Lower-relevance items

- `cross-regime` · `national:FR` — 2025-04-25, Générations Futures responded to ANSES alerts by demanding that "all products (plant protection, biocidal or veterinary)" based on deltamethrin "and more generally on a pyrethroid substance" be withdrawn from the market. This is class-level: it names deltamethrin and cypermethrin, not metofluthrin. It is the only NGO demand found that would logically cover metofluthrin biocides. https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/alerte-anses-pesticides/
- `BPR` · `national:DE` — 2025-06-11, PAN Germany store check (March–April 2025, 130+ shops) on Germany's new ban on self-service sales of biocidal insect sprays. PAN Germany criticises insecticides being relabelled as "repellents" and asks for enforcement checks and a consumer complaint channel. This is class-level: it names chrysanthemum extract, pyrethrins and pyrethroids in general, not metofluthrin. https://pan-germany.org/biozide/insektensprays-co-greifen-die-neuen-verkaufsregeln-im-handel/
- `water` · `national:DE` — 2025-11-20, PAN Germany article on pyrethroids being extremely toxic to aquatic organisms. It calls for less pyrethroid use, lower emissions to surface water, better monitoring and harmonised pyrethroid regulation, and mentions biocidal pest-control uses. This is class-level: it lists ten pyrethroids by name (e.g. permethrin, deltamethrin, cypermethrin), and metofluthrin is not one of them. https://pan-germany.org/pestizide/extrem-toxisch-fuer-wasserorganismen-pyrethroid-insektizide-im-fokus-von-wissenschaft-und-regulation/
- `international` — PAN Pesticide Database (PAN North America) entry for metofluthrin: undated database record, not activity. It says metofluthrin is not on the PAN HHP list and lists endocrine disruption and carcinogenicity as "insufficiently studied". https://www.pesticideinfo.org/chemical/PRI4196/

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

Nothing has been announced about metofluthrin or epsilon-metofluthrin specifically. At class level:
- Générations Futures continues to push for pyrethroids to be withdrawn in all regimes, including biocides, in France.

Either campaign could later take in metofluthrin-based mosquito products, but neither has done so yet.
