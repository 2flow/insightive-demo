- `cross-regime` · `national:US` — 2026-10-08 (pre-window, missed earlier): Beyond Pesticides blog post "Widely Used Insecticide In and Around Homes Found To Damage Muscles in Overlooked Biochemical Pathway". It names transfluthrin and reports an in-vitro study in *Toxicology* (myotubes) that found oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, atrophy and altered glucose use.
