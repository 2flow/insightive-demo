## 1. Summary

None of the top signals target the biocide approval, and no top signals were found in any regime. No NGO page in the window names transfluthrin. The only related NGO activity is at pyrethroid-class level. In April 2025, Générations Futures responded to an ANSES alert on prenatal pyrethroid exposure by asking for all pyrethroid products to be withdrawn quickly, including biocidal and veterinary ones. In November 2025, PAN Germany campaigned on pyrethroid toxicity in surface waters. Neither names transfluthrin. NGOs have not reacted publicly to transfluthrin's EU carcinogenicity classification (CLH opinion adopted) or to the Union authorisation of transfluthrin emanators. Pressure on the BPR renewal is therefore low and only indirect, through possible pyrethroid-wide campaigns.

## 3. Lower-relevance items

- `cross-regime` · `national:FR` — 2025-04-25: Générations Futures asks for all pyrethroid-based products (agricultural, biocidal and veterinary) to be removed from the market quickly. It cites an ANSES alert on behavioural disorders in children exposed to pyrethroids before birth. The page is class-level and names deltamethrin and cypermethrin, not transfluthrin. It is still the closest NGO demand to the transfluthrin biocide file, because it explicitly covers household and biocidal pyrethroids and rests on hazard grounds. https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/alerte-anses-pesticides/
- `water` · `national:DE` — 2025-11-20: PAN Germany asks for less pyrethroid use, group-based environmental quality standards and mixture-toxicity indicators for pyrethroids in surface waters, and mentions biocidal uses (material protection, pest control). The page is class-level and does not name transfluthrin. https://pan-germany.org/wasser/extrem-toxisch-fuer-wasserorganismen-pyrethroid-insektizide-im-fokus-von-wissenschaft-und-regulation/
- `BPR` · `national:FR` — undated: UFC-Que Choisir's buying guide on anti-mosquito products names transfluthrin as an example of a pyrethroid. It says such insecticides use substances harmful to health and should be used sparingly. It is consumer advice, not a campaign, and the date could not be confirmed (unverified for the window). https://www.quechoisir.org/guide-d-achat-antimoustiques-n7841/
