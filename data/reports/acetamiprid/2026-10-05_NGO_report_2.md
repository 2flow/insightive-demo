# NGO activity report: Acetamiprid (CAS 135410-20-7), BPR PT18

## 1. Summary

Of the 8 top signals, 5 target the PPP approval or national PPP derogations, 1 is cross-regime (the Omnibus "unlimited approvals" fight) and 2 target the biocide approval directly. Both direct signals come from Générations Futures (France). The most important one, dated 2025-09-30, says the manufacturer filed no BPR renewal application by the 2025-07-31 deadline. Générations Futures expects household biocides with acetamiprid to leave the market from 2027 and calls for a total ban on agricultural and biocide uses. At EU level, PAN Europe, Générations Futures and BeeLife keep pushing the Commission. They want acetamiprid suspended, classified Repr. 1B and treated as a candidate for substitution (letter of 2025-05-21). In the 2026-05-20 World Bee Day letter to Commissioners Várhelyi and Roswall, they also ask for an EFSA bee/wild-bee mandate, and they point out that the BPR dossier calls the substance "very persistent". In France, the most intense activity over the last 6 months was the campaign against the "Duplomb 2" provisions (Senate in June 2026, Assemblée vote on 2026-07-21, Conseil constitutionnel decision on 2026-08-14). Those provisions let Anses grant hazelnut derogations for acetamiprid. NGOs have said they will go to court if any derogation is granted. The overall direction is consistent pressure to remove acetamiprid in every regime. The BPR file itself looks set to lapse because no renewal application was filed (as the NGOs describe it), so NGO pressure would mainly matter if anyone tried to revive or extend the biocide approval.

## 2. Top signals (ranked by likely impact on the BPR renewal)

### Tier A: direct BPR signals

**1. 2025-09-30: Générations Futures says acetamiprid will not be re-approved as a biocide because no renewal was requested, and demands an EU-wide ban on all agricultural and biocide uses, citing neurotoxicity and endocrine-disruption concerns**
`BPR` · `EU` · `direct` · `statement`
- Summary: Générations Futures reports that the manufacturer (Nisso Chemical Europe) made no request to renew acetamiprid's BPR approval before the 2025-07-31 deadline. As a result, household insecticides with acetamiprid will progressively disappear from 2027, with complete discontinuation by early 2028. The NGO notes that Nisso is still running supplementary ED/DNT studies for the pesticide file. It argues that industry "knew acetamiprid had no chance of being re-authorised". It calls for a total ban on agricultural and biocide uses in France and the EU.
- Why it matters: This is the only NGO item that speaks directly to the state of the BPR renewal. As the NGO describes it, there is no renewal to fight. Its importance is that NGOs are watching the file and would publicly oppose any late application, extension or derogation. The reasoning rests on hazard (DNT, ED), which carries over strongly.
- NGO: Générations Futures. https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/acetamipride-biocide-duplomb/

**2. 2025-05-22: Générations Futures fact-checks the French agriculture minister and stresses that the 213 acetamiprid household biocides in France have never been assessed and are unlikely to be approved under the stricter toxicity values**
`BPR` · `national:FR` · `direct` · `statement`
- Summary: In reply to ministerial claims that acetamiprid is "in all household insecticides and flea collars", Générations Futures counted 213 acetamiprid biocidal products in France. It says all of them have "pending application" or "transitional" status with Anses, so none has been evaluated at product level. It adds that the revised (lower) toxicity reference values make broad product approval unlikely. It also found that no authorised veterinary flea collar in France contains acetamiprid.
- Why it matters: This targets biocidal products directly and argues that the 2024 reduction in toxicological reference values (a hazard-based change) should block product authorisations. It shows that French NGOs use the biocide market as a talking point in the PPP debate.
- NGO: Générations Futures. https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/acetamipride-antipuce-genevard/

### Tier B: EU-level hazard signals (indirect, carry over strongly)

**3. 2026-05-20: Bee NGOs ask the EU for a new safety check on acetamiprid's risk to bees, including wild bees, and point out that the EU's own biocide assessment calls it very persistent**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: PAN Europe and BeeLife wrote to Commissioners Várhelyi and Roswall for World Bee Day. They say acetamiprid is "persistent in the environment and toxic to humans, birds and bees", and they flag that "the biocide dossier for acetamiprid characterises it as 'very persistent in the environment', while the pesticide dossier ... concludes that it is non-persistent". They welcome the Article 21 review on DNT/ED (EFSA mandate of May 2025) but ask for a separate EFSA mandate on bees and non-target arthropods based on the 2023 Bee Guidance Document. They also use acetamiprid as an example against the Omnibus's open-ended approvals.
- Why it matters: This is the only top signal that explicitly uses the BPR assessment (the vP conclusion) as evidence against the substance in another regime. It is hazard-based, so any BPR re-examination would face the same argument.
- NGO: PAN Europe and BeeLife. https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/20260520%20World%20Bee%20Day%20Letter%20PAN%20Europe%20and%20BeeLife%20to%20Com.%20Roswall%20and%20Varhelyi.pdf ; BeeLife post: https://www.bee-life.eu/post/joint-letter-to-the-european-commission-protecting-pollinators-requires-action

**4. 2026-02-05: PAN and Générations Futures warn that the EU "Omnibus" would give acetamiprid and 48 other pesticides unlimited approval, citing its toxicity to the foetal brain**
`cross-regime` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `study/report`
- Summary: Générations Futures' analysis of the Food and Feed Safety Omnibus proposal finds that 49 synthetic pesticides, acetamiprid among them, would get open-ended approvals if the proposal takes effect on 2027-01-01. The NGOs oppose ending periodic renewals and have asked the Commission to confirm the list of substances.
- Why it matters: This is a horizontal policy fight. If the NGOs win, periodic re-evaluation stays and acetamiprid's PPP file remains open to hazard-based review, which feeds into how the substance is seen in all regimes. The effect on the BPR is indirect.
- NGO: PAN Europe and Générations Futures. https://www.pan-europe.info/press-releases/2026/02/pesticide-action-network-raises-alarm-over-49-pesticides-facing-unlimited

**5. 2025-05-21: PAN Europe and Générations Futures ask the Commission to suspend acetamiprid at once and classify it as toxic to reproduction (Cat. 1B) and a candidate for substitution, citing 16 new studies on harm to the developing brain**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: In a letter to DG SANTE, the NGOs present 16 peer-reviewed studies on developmental neurotoxicity and ask for immediate suspension of the EU approval. The press release contrasts the 7-year biocide approval (to 2027) with the 15-year PPP approval (to 2033).
- Why it matters: A Repr. 1B classification would be a hazard trigger under both CLP and the BPR exclusion criteria, so this demand carries over strongly. The Article 21 DNT/ED review opened in May 2025 follows the direction of this request (as PAN Europe describes it in the 2026-05-20 letter).
- NGO: PAN Europe and Générations Futures. https://www.pan-europe.info/press-releases/2025/05/pan-europe-and-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rations-futures-ask-european-commission-ban-neurotoxic ; letter: https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/20250520_Letter%20S.Gallina_New%20scientific%20evidence%20on%20acetamiprid.pdf

**6. 2025-05-20: Générations Futures publishes a science dossier arguing acetamiprid should be banned in France and the EU, citing developmental neurotoxicity, endocrine disruption, persistence in water and unacceptable risks from biocide uses**
`PPP` · `national:FR` · `indirect–hazard` · `study/report`
- Summary: The dossier was written to counter the "Duplomb" bill. It cites a water half-life of 79.7 days, 23 recent studies on bee toxicity (including solitary bees and bumblebees), DNT concerns, and an OECD 426 study that has been outstanding for 12 years. It also argues that approved biocide formulations pose unacceptable risks to professional users and children.
- Why it matters: It is aimed mainly at the French PPP derogation, but it rests on hazard grounds and explicitly criticises biocidal uses. It is a ready-made evidence base for any BPR-related challenge.
- NGO: Générations Futures. https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/acetamipride-duplomb/

### Tier C: national use-specific signals (indirect, carry over weakly)

**7. 2026-08-25: French beekeepers and environmental NGOs fight the new law that lets Anses grant acetamiprid derogations for hazelnuts, win a "silence means refusal" safeguard, and promise court challenges**
`PPP` · `national:FR` · `indirect–use` · `formal request`
- Summary: UNAF filed a submission to the Conseil constitutionnel against the loi d'urgence agricole. It reports that the Council's 2026-08-14 ruling means any derogation request is deemed rejected if Anses does not decide within two months. Générations Futures (2026-08-14) and POLLINIS (2026-09-03) say that responsibility now lies with Anses and that they will take legal action if derogations are granted. This followed months of campaigning (Senate committee 2026-06-17, Senate vote 2026-06-30, UNAF action at the Assemblée 2026-07-21).
- Why it matters: This concerns use-specific national PPP derogations, so it carries over weakly to the BPR. It shows that the French NGO coalition is mobilised and ready to litigate on acetamiprid.
- NGO: UNAF https://www.unaf-apiculture.info/communique-de-presse/ ; Générations Futures https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/decision-conseil-constitutionnel-neonicotinoides/ ; POLLINIS https://www.pollinis.org/publications/loi-durgence-agricole-le-conseil-constitutionnel-donne-a-lanses-la-cle-pour-empecher-le-retour-des-neonicotinoides/

**8. 2025-08-05: German beekeepers and BUND Naturschutz call for an end to the record number of emergency authorisations for acetamiprid sprays, citing nerve toxicity and residues in food and honey**
`PPP` · `national:DE` · `indirect–use` · `campaign`
- Summary: The Bündnis für eine neonikotinoidfreie Landwirtschaft (BUND Naturschutz in Bayern, DBIB beekeepers) criticises more than 100 emergency authorisations in Germany in 2025, 10 of them for acetamiprid. It demands an end to open-field emergency authorisations, public reporting of where products are applied, and crop rotation instead. DBIB followed up on 2025-08-28 and noted the EU increase of the honey MRL from 0.05 to 0.3 mg/kg.
- Why it matters: These are use-specific PPP derogations and carry over weakly. They show sustained acetamiprid pressure in Germany, a large market for both PPP and biocides.
- NGO: BUND Naturschutz in Bayern https://www.bund-naturschutz.de/pressemitteilungen/neonicotinoide-lebensmittel-stark-mit-hochwirksamem-nervengift-belastet ; DBIB https://berufsimker.de/schilfglasfluegelzikade-immer-mehr-notfallpestizid-ohne-notfall/

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

- **BPR phase-out:** Générations Futures expects acetamiprid household biocides to disappear progressively from 2027, with full discontinuation by early 2028, because no renewal application was filed. It wants the same outcome for agricultural uses.
- **Anses hazelnut derogation decisions (France):** after the 2026-08-14 Conseil constitutionnel decision, Anses has two months to decide on each derogation request, and silence counts as refusal. Générations Futures, UNAF and POLLINIS say they will challenge any granted derogation in the administrative courts.
- **EU Article 21 review (DNT/ED):** EFSA's review under the May 2025 mandate is pending. PAN Europe and BeeLife want a second mandate covering bees, including wild bees, under the 2023 Bee Guidance Document, and a decision on the vP/non-persistent inconsistency between the BPR and PPP dossiers.
- **Omnibus:** PAN Europe, Générations Futures and Friends of the Earth Europe are campaigning against open-ended approvals (proposed entry into force on 2027-01-01) and use acetamiprid as their main example.
- **Germany:** the beekeeper/BUND alliance continues to oppose seasonal emergency authorisations for acetamiprid sprays.
