## 1. Summary

0 of 0 top signals: no NGO item in the window (2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06) targets kieselguhr (diatomaceous earth) in any regime, and none targets the biocide approval. NGOs that mention it do so favourably. PAN Germany, PAN UK, Beyond Pesticides and Xerces present it as a low-toxicity, physical-mode alternative to synthetic insecticides. The only caution they raise is about use: Beyond Pesticides tells users to buy food-grade (amorphous) DE and avoid calcined "pool-grade" DE, which contains crystalline silica. The NGO direction is neutral to supportive, and there is no sign of NGO pressure ahead of the 2028 BPR expiry.
