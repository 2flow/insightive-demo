**2026-06-24 — PAN Europe asks EU Member States to reject renewing phosphine as a pesticide, citing EFSA's finding that it is genotoxic with no safe threshold**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: In its position letter for the 29–30 June 2026 SCoPAFF meeting (agenda item B.09), PAN Europe calls on Member States to reject the Commission's proposal to renew phosphine. It cites EFSA's critical area of concern on genotoxicity, which rests on in vitro, in vivo (somatic cells) and human biomonitoring data. It notes that no threshold mode of action and no reference values could be set, and invokes Annex II point 3.6.2 ("mutagenic substances must not be approved under any circumstances"). This is the third letter in a series; the two earlier letters (2025-12-08 and 2026-04-30) are listed under lower relevance. The letter names only "phosphine"; aluminium phosphide, magnesium phosphide, hydrogen cyanide and sulfuryl fluoride do not appear.
- Why it matters: The argument rests on hazard grounds and attaches to phosphine itself. Phosphine is the active toxic agent of both BPR phosphine generators on the watch list (aluminium phosphide and magnesium phosphide releasing phosphine), so a genotoxicity or mutagenicity argument carries over strongly to their PT18/PT14 assessments and to any CLH discussion. The PPP renewal reportedly went ahead with strict mitigation conditions and a confirmatory genotoxicity data requirement due by 2028 (non-NGO source). The hazard finding remains on record for the BPR evaluations.
- Named substances: none
- NGO: PAN Europe, [SCoPAFF position letter, 29–30 June 2026 (PDF)](https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/2026_06_29-30%20-%20SCoPAFF%20letter.pdf)
