# NGO activity on Imiprothrin (CAS 72963-72-5), 2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06

## 1. Summary

1 top signal, which is cross-regime and international; none target the biocide approval directly, and none fall in the past 6 months (since 2026-04-06). I found no NGO campaign, letter, legal action or testing in the window that names imiprothrin. The only substance-specific NGO item is a standing one: PAN International lists imiprothrin as a Highly Hazardous Pesticide because it is highly toxic to bees. The list was re-issued in December 2024, but imiprothrin has been on it since 2013. NGO pressure on household insecticides in the EU is real but stays at class level. In Germany, PAN Germany and Verbraucherzentrale NRW are pushing on pyrethroid insect sprays and their sales rules. PAN Netherlands has a "black list" of household insecticides, and imiprothrin is not on it. PAN Europe is pursuing cypermethrin and the Omnibus. None of these name imiprothrin. Direction: flat. Imiprothrin is not currently an NGO target.

## 3. Lower-relevance items

- `BPR` · `national:DE` (2025-06-11): PAN Germany and Verbraucherzentrale NRW tested 130+ shops to see whether Germany's ban on self-service sales of insect sprays (in force since January 2025) is being followed. They call for enforcement and for closing the "repellent" loophole. Class-level: names pyrethroids, pyrethrins and PBO but not imiprothrin. https://pan-germany.org/biozide/insektensprays-co-greifen-die-neuen-verkaufsregeln-im-handel/ and https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/pressemitteilungen/insektenspray-beim-kauf-jetzt-besser-beraten-107953
- `water` · `EU` (2025-11-20): PAN Germany highlights a scientific review calling for less pyrethroid use, group-based water quality standards and harmonised regulation of pyrethroids across PPP, biocides and medicines. Class-level: lists agricultural pyrethroids plus permethrin and bifenthrin, not imiprothrin. https://pan-germany.org/pestizide/extrem-toxisch-fuer-wasserorganismen-pyrethroid-insektizide-im-fokus-von-wissenschaft-und-regulation/
- `BPR` · `national:NL` (2025-04-07): PAN Netherlands publishes a "black list" of 10 insecticides sold to private users (deltamethrin, flupyradifurone, imidacloprid, lambda-cyhalothrin, permethrin, prallethrin, pyrethrin, spinosad, tetramethrin, transfluthrin) and calls for a ban on harmful pesticides for non-professional use. Class-level: imiprothrin is not on the list, and I could not text-search the report itself (unverified for any mention). https://www.pan-netherlands.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/onderzoeksrapport.pdf
- `PPP` · `EU` (2025-12 and 2026-07): PAN Europe and BeeLife won the cypermethrin court case and are now pressing the Commission over the delayed ban. Relevant only as a pyrethroid precedent; does not name imiprothrin. https://www.pan-europe.info/press-releases/2026/07/european-commission-delays-ban-cypermethrin-despite-eu-court-judgement
- `cross-regime` · `EU` (2026-06): PAN Europe warns that the pesticide Omnibus's draft "biocontrol" definition could take in synthetic pyrethroids. Class-level, does not name imiprothrin. https://www.pan-europe.info/press-releases/2026/06/new-science-warning-raises-alarms-over-eu-pesticide-omnibus
- Out of window, for context only: GLOBAL 2000 shopping tests of household pest-control products (2016, 2018) named imiprothrin products (e.g. Raid ant spray). US NGO comments on EPA pyrethroid registration review (2017 to 2020). Friends of the Earth US pollinator-toxicity list (2020-04) includes imiprothrin.

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

None of them say anything about imiprothrin specifically. Class-level things to watch:
- PAN Germany and Verbraucherzentrale NRW want enforcement of Germany's insect-spray sales rules and the "repellent" loophole closed.

- PAN Europe is following up on the cypermethrin judgment and opposing the Omnibus.
