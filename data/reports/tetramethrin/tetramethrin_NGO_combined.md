`BPR` · `national:NL` · `direct` · `study/report`

- **Summary:** PAN-NL surveyed insecticides sold to consumers online and in garden centres, such as ant baits, wasp sprays and powders. Its report "PAN-NL zet 10 insecticiden op de zwarte lijst" names 10 substances, including tetramethrin, that it says should at minimum be banned for private, non-professional use: deltamethrin, flupyradifurone, imidacloprid, lambda-cyhalothrin, permethrin, prallethrin, pyrethrin, spinosad, tetramethrin and transfluthrin. The list is aimed at retailers, garden centres and citizens. PAN-NL notes that some of these substances are banned in agriculture but still allowed in consumer products.
- **Why it matters for the BPR approval:** It acts directly, because it targets biocidal products containing tetramethrin. Its reasons are hazard-based: endocrine disruption, neurotoxicity, reproductive toxicity and toxicity to insects and aquatic life. These line up with the open ED assessment for tetramethrin under the BPR and could feed into comparative assessment or restrictions on consumer use at product-authorisation stage (Ctgb). It is a national campaign that does not challenge the EU active-substance approval itself, and no follow-up naming tetramethrin was found. Impact is low to moderate.
