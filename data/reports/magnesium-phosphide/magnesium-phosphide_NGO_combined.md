**2.1 2026-06-24: PAN Europe asks EU Member States to vote against renewing the grain fumigant phosphine, saying EFSA found it may damage DNA and EU law bans mutagenic pesticides**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`

- **Summary:** In its position letter before the 29–30 June 2026 SCoPAFF meeting, PAN Europe asked Member States to reject the Commission's draft regulation renewing phosphine (agenda item B.09). It cites EFSA's peer-review conclusion that phosphine's genotoxic potential is a critical area of concern, based on in vitro, in vivo (somatic cell) and human biomonitoring data. It says no threshold mode of action or reference values could be set, and that Annex II point 3.6.2 bars mutagenic substances from approval "under any circumstances".
- **Why it matters for the BPR renewal:** This is indirect, but it carries over strongly because it rests on hazard. Phosphine is the substance that magnesium phosphide biocides release. A genotoxicity concern with no safe threshold, if formalised (for example through PPP non-renewal or CLH), would weigh directly on the BPR exclusion and risk assessment for magnesium phosphide. This is the most recent and most pointed of the three letters, as it came at the vote stage.
