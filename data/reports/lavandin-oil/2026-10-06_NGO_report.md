## 1. Summary

None of the top signals target the biocide approval or any other regime, because no NGO activity on lavandin oil (or lavender oil as a substance) was found in the window 2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06. Environmental, health and consumer NGOs do not campaign against lavandin oil. Where it comes up in NGO material at all (e.g. older PAN Germany biocide guides), it is listed as a low-risk natural repellent for moths and textile pests, which is the opposite of opposition. The public argument over lavender and lavandin essential oils under the CLP/chemicals-strategy revision (2022–2023) came from growers, farm unions and producer petitions, not from NGOs, and it falls before the window. Overall direction: no NGO pressure.
