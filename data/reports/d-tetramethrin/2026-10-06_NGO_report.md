## 1. Summary

The single top signal targets biocide products directly (1 of 1 top signals is BPR, national, NL). No NGO item in the window names d-tetramethrin specifically. NGOs use the generic name "tetramethrin", and only one, PAN Netherlands, did anything substantive with it. In April 2025 PAN-NL published an online market survey and a "black list" of 10 insecticides. The list includes tetramethrin in consumer wasp and silverfish sprays sold at Welkoop and Praxis. PAN-NL wants these products phased out of retail by 2027 and wants the Dutch government to ban synthetic pesticides for non-professional use. Everything else is class-level pyrethroid work that does not name tetramethrin (PAN Germany, Beyond Pesticides) or older material from before the window. No NGO has campaigned on the EU active-substance evaluation itself, run litigation or made a formal request. Overall NGO pressure on the BPR file is low. Because PAN-NL rests its case on hazard (endocrine disruption, neurotoxicity, carcinogenicity), its arguments could still feed into the open ED assessment and the candidate-for-substitution decision.

## 2. Top signals

### Tier A: direct

**2025-04-07: PAN Netherlands asks retailers to stop selling home-and-garden insect sprays containing tetramethrin and the government to ban such products for consumers, saying tetramethrin is endocrine-disrupting and possibly neurotoxic and carcinogenic**
`BPR` · `national:NL` · `direct` · `campaign`

- Summary: PAN-NL ran an online survey of pesticides sold for private use. It named Luxan and Protect Home silverfish sprays and the Luxan wasp spray (Welkoop), plus Praxis products, as containing permethrin and tetramethrin. The report says the Ctgb has authorised 33 tetramethrin products as biocides. It places tetramethrin on a "ZWARTE LIJST" (black list) of 10 insecticides alongside deltamethrin, imidacloprid, permethrin, prallethrin, transfluthrin and others. It asks retailers and webshops to offer no such products to private users by 2027, and asks the government to regulate toward a ban on synthetic pesticides for non-professional use. The report states that tetramethrin "heeft een hormoonverstorende werking, het is mogelijk neurotoxisch en kankerverwekkend". The launch was timed with a broadcast on the AVROTROS consumer programme RADAR (NPO2) on the same day.
- Why it matters: This is the only item that targets biocidal products containing the substance, so it acts directly, though only at national level and on the consumer market rather than on the EU approval decision. Its arguments are hazard-based (ED, neurotoxicity, carcinogenicity), and those carry over strongly. They match the open BPR ED assessment and the pending candidate-for-substitution assessment. However, PAN-NL sources these hazards from the PPDB database, not from new evidence. The likely impact is more on retail listings and Dutch product authorisations of non-professional products than on the active-substance approval.
- NGO: PAN Netherlands. [Research report (PDF, dated 7 April 2025)](https://www.pan-netherlands.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/onderzoeksrapport.pdf) · [Press release (PDF)](https://www.pan-netherlands.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/persbericht.pdf)
