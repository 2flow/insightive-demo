## 1. Summary

0 of 0 top signals: no NGO found in the window campaigns on, litigates over, formally challenges or commissions testing of Icaridin in any regime (BPR, PPP, REACH/CLP, water, food). What exists is consumer advice. German groups describe Icaridin as the "more tolerable" alternative to DEET. One of them, Umweltinstitut München, also warns that Icaridin repellents harm newt larvae once they wash into water, but its page carries no date and makes no regulatory demand. US NGOs (EWG, Beyond Pesticides) rate picaridin favourably as a lower-toxicity alternative to DEET. Overall, NGOs are not pushing against the BPR approval. The only plausible future trigger is the growing academic evidence on aquatic and amphibian toxicity and on persistence, which NGOs have so far only echoed in consumer tips.
