## 3. Lower-relevance items

- `cross-regime` · `international`: Beyond Pesticides' "Pesticide Gateway" factsheet rates Prallethrin "Toxic". It lists possible cancer, likely endocrine disruption, neurotoxicity, kidney/liver effects, sensitiser/irritant, and toxicity to fish, aquatic organisms and bees. This is a standing database entry with no date, so it is not a dated event in the window. https://www.beyondpesticides.org/resources/pesticide-gateway?pesticideid=405
- `cross-regime` · `international`: PAN's PesticideInfo database (PAN North America) marks Prallethrin as a PAN "Highly Hazardous Pesticide" and as a bee toxicant. Reproductive/developmental and endocrine effects are marked "insufficiently studied". This is an undated database entry. The current PAN International HHP List PDF (updated December 2024) could not be machine-read to confirm the listing directly. https://www.pesticideinfo.org/chemical/PRI5323/
- `cross-regime` · `EU`: Class-level only, does not name Prallethrin. PAN Europe's Food and Feed Safety Omnibus page (December 2025, updated 2026) warns that the proposed biocontrol definition could cover synthetic "pyrethroids". https://www.pan-europe.info/eu-legislation/food-and-feed-safety-omnibus-weaker-protection
- `cross-regime` · `national:US`: The "Poisoning Our Children" blog (2026-08-10) lists Prallethrin among pyrethroids that "should almost never be used except in cases of real Public Health emergency". It concerns US mosquito spraying. This looks like an individual's advocacy blog, not an NGO, so its NGO status is unclear. https://www.poisoningchildren.com/blog/2026/8/10/beware-of-mosquito-spraying

Outside the window, noted for context only: GLOBAL 2000's biocides background paper (May 2016) discusses a Prallethrin-containing household insect spray and its hazard labelling (https://www.global2000.at/sites/global/files/Report%20Biozide.pdf). Consumentenbond (NL) advised against anti-mosquito plug-ins in 2019 on pyrethroid irritation grounds, without naming Prallethrin.
