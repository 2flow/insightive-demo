Nothing specific to Bacillus sphaericus. One thing to watch: German conservation NGOs keep criticising area-wide Bti larviciding in protected wetlands. If that criticism widens to cover all microbial mosquito larvicides, B. sphaericus products (often sold as Bti + B. sphaericus combinations) could be drawn in on use-specific (non-target Diptera / food-web) grounds. That would carry over weakly to the BPR file. No such move was found.
