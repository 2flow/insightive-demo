## 1. Summary

None of the top signals target the biocide approval, and there are no top signals in any regime. In the 2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06 window I found no verified NGO campaign, letter, legal action or study that names pyriproxyfen. When NGOs mention it at all, it is one entry in a residue table or a hazard list: PAN Netherlands' garden-plant testing, foodwatch's residue report and GLOBAL 2000's retailer residue limits. None of these mentions could be confirmed from the NGO's own page. The NGO campaigns closest to its uses do not name pyriproxyfen. These are the UK coalition on pet flea and tick treatments (WildFish, Buglife and others, April 2026), which names only fipronil and imidacloprid, and the French and Dutch residue campaigns against endocrine-disrupting and highly hazardous pesticides. The older debates that linked pyriproxyfen to microcephaly in Brazil and to endocrine disruption (2016–2021) have not come back in NGO material during the window. Overall, NGOs are not putting pressure on the PT18 renewal.
