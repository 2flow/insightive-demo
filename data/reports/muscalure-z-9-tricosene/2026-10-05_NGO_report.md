## 1. Summary

No NGO activity on Muscalure ((Z)-9-tricosene) was found in the window (2024-10-05 to 2026-10-05), and none in the focus period from 2026-04-05. Searches of the named European NGOs and of several others turned up no press releases, reports, campaigns, testing or litigation that name the substance. Muscalure is a housefly sex pheromone used as an attractant in traps, strips and baits. In NGO and consumer writing, pheromone-based traps usually come up as a lower-risk alternative to insecticides, not as a target. The overall trend is neutral: NGOs are putting no pressure on the BPR renewal.
