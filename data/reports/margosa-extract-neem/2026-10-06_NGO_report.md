# NGO activity report: Margosa extract (neem), CAS 84696-25-3

## 1. Summary

None of the top signals target the biocide approval, because there are no top signals. No NGO item in the window (2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06) targets the biocide approval, the plant protection approval (as azadirachtin) or any other regime. NGOs that mention neem or azadirachtin at all treat it as a low-hazard botanical alternative to synthetic insecticides, not as a target. The only in-window lead is an organic-sector position paper (IFOAM Organics Europe, May 2025). Search results say it cites azadirachtin as a needed biocontrol tool for organic fruit growing, so it argues for keeping the substance. I could not open the paper to confirm this. NGO pressure on the PT19 approval (expires 2029-03-31) and on the pending PT18 application is effectively nil.

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

Nothing specific to Margosa extract (neem). In the wider policy debate, organic-sector groups (IFOAM Organics Europe, statement of 2025-12-18 on the Food and Feed Safety Omnibus) are pushing for a separate, faster approval route for biocontrol substances. That would, if anything, favour botanical actives such as azadirachtin under PPP law, though the statement does not name it. No NGO has announced litigation, testing or campaigns on neem.
