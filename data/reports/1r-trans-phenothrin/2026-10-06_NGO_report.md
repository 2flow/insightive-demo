## 1. Summary

There are no top signals: no NGO item in the window names 1R-trans phenothrin (or phenothrin or sumithrin) and targets the EU biocide approval, or any other EU regime. In the EU, the closest pressure is class-level. In April 2025 Générations Futures asked for every pyrethroid product to be taken off the French market, explicitly including biocides and veterinary products, after ANSES health alerts on pyrethroids. The NGO named only deltamethrin and cypermethrin. The only NGO pages that name phenothrin are from the US: a Massachusetts coalition campaigning against wide-area mosquito spraying, and Beyond Pesticides' hazard database. Neither bears on the EU file. Overall, NGOs are not focused on this substance, but the general push against pyrethroids (neurodevelopmental, endocrine and aquatic-toxicity concerns) could later spread to all pyrethroid biocides, including this one.

## 3. Lower-relevance items

This is class-level: phenothrin is not named. Its campaign page names phenothrin (Sumithrin) as a mosquito adulticide that "can result in lung irritation, and has been documented to cause asthmatic responses".
