## 3. Lower-relevance items

It advises readers to look for "hydrated cyclized", "PMD Rich Botanic Oil" or the CAS number on the label. The article only assesses efficacy and makes no regulatory demand, so it reads as favourable to the substance rather than as pressure on it.
