## 1. Summary

Of the 9 top signals, 4 target the PPP approval or PPP products, 2 are cross-regime, 2 concern water quality, and 1 targets the biocides regime. 3 signals (PAN Europe 2025, Générations Futures 2025 and 2026) ask for action on biocidal deltamethrin directly. Most of the activity rests on one hazard argument: newer animal studies that NGOs say show developmental neurotoxicity (brain damage in offspring) at or below the EU "safe" dose (NOAEL), plus suspected thyroid and endocrine effects and very high aquatic toxicity. PAN Europe leads at EU level. Its February 2025 letter to DG SANTE (Ms Gallina) and SCoPAFF asked for an Article 21 review and a ban, and in April–May 2026 it fought the tenth PPP extension, to June 2028, using the General Court's case law on extensions. In April 2026 the push became national litigation when Deutsche Umwelthilfe sued Germany's BVL over two deltamethrin products. Over the past six months pressure has gone up: litigation in Germany, PAN Germany at Bayer's AGM, a Swiss water-protection coalition, and local French opposition to mosquito spraying. The BPR file has not yet been the main target, but the hazard case NGOs are building would carry straight over to it.

## 2. Top signals

### Tier A: direct

**2026-03-06 — Générations Futures urges the EU to keep regular safety re-checks for biocides under the "Omnibus" simplification, and names deltamethrin as a heavily used biocidal insecticide linked to child behavioural disorders**
`BPR` · `EU` · `direct` · `statement`
- Summary: The French NGO criticises the Omnibus X proposal to give 34 biocidal active substances unlimited approvals (Article 15a) and asks for periodic review to stay, more resources for competent authorities and complete industry dossiers. It cites ANSES's May 2025 alert on prenatal pyrethroid exposure and says deltamethrin and cypermethrin are "still heavily used" in professional and consumer biocides. Deltamethrin is not on GF's list of the 34 substances.
- Why it matters: It targets the BPR regime itself and names deltamethrin's biocidal uses, so it is direct, though as a horizontal policy campaign rather than a challenge to the substance file. It rests on hazard grounds (prenatal neurodevelopmental effects), which carry over strongly to the eCA's (Sweden's) assessment of human health.
- NGO: Générations Futures. [Produits biocides: Générations Futures pointe les limites de la réglementation actuelle…](https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/produit-biocides-generations-futures-pointe-les-limites-de-la-reglementation-actuelle-et-alerte-sur-les-risques-de-deregulation-dans-le-cadre-du-projet-domnibus-x/)

**2025-04-25 — Générations Futures demands that all deltamethrin and pyrethroid products (farm, household biocide and veterinary) be withdrawn quickly, citing ANSES's warning on brain-development risks for children exposed in the womb**
`cross-regime` · `national:FR` · `direct` · `statement`
- Summary: Responding to ANSES phytopharmacovigilance alerts, GF says pyrethroids including deltamethrin disrupt foetal brain development even at low doses. It points to agricultural, domestic (household insecticide) and veterinary exposure routes, and asks for market withdrawal "within a short timeframe" across all product types.
- Why it matters: It explicitly covers biocidal products, so it is direct. It rests on hazard grounds that carry over strongly, and it amplifies an ANSES signal that ANSES itself says needs agricultural and biocidal sources separated, which could feed French input to the BPR renewal.
- NGO: Générations Futures. [Pyréthrinoïdes et autres pesticides : des signaux d'alerte clairs sur la santé…](https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/alerte-anses-pesticides/)

**2025-02-26 — PAN Europe asks the Commission for an emergency ban on deltamethrin and says it should also be prohibited as a biocide, citing studies showing brain damage in offspring at the EU's "safe" dose** (TOP SIGNAL)
`cross-regime` · `EU` · `direct` · `formal request`
- Summary: The letter to DG SANTE (Ms Gallina) and SCoPAFF members urges an immediate Article 21 review of the PPP approval and a ban. Its annex lists about 30 studies on developmental neurotoxicity, thyroid and neuroendocrine effects, reproductive toxicity and immunotoxicity, including two 2025 mouse studies at the 1 ppm NOAEL. It also asks for mandatory developmental neurotoxicity (DNT) testing and states: "Deltamethrin should also be prohibited as a biocide, considering the important amounts that are sprayed through aerial spraying for mosquito control in some Member States." PAN Europe publicised the letter in a press release on 2025-03-04, and it underpins PAN Europe's "Save our brain" campaign page on deltamethrin.
- Why it matters: This is the only NGO document found that explicitly asks for deltamethrin's biocide approval to end. It is hazard-based (DNT and endocrine, at or below the NOAEL), so it carries over strongly to the BPR renewal: the same reference values and ED questions will come up in the Swedish eCA's evaluation, and NGOs can be expected to submit this dossier in any BPR public consultation.
- NGO: PAN Europe. [Letter "Emergency ban for pesticide deltamethrin needed" (PDF)](https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/Stop%20use%20of%20deltamethrin.pdf); [Press release 2025-03-04](https://www.pan-europe.info/press-releases/2025/03/pesticide-deltamethrin-causes-brain-damage-offspring-safe-dose); [Campaign page](https://www.pan-europe.info/campaigns/save-our-brain/deltamethrin)

### Tier B: indirect, hazard-based

**2026-05-13 — PAN Europe tells the Commission that its own court wins make repeated extensions of substances like deltamethrin unlawful, because their approvals have "more than doubled"**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: The letter to Deputy Director-General Claire Bury draws lessons from rulings in PAN Europe's cases, including T-412/22 (19 November 2025): approval extensions must be "temporary and exceptional" and cannot run far past the original approval period. It names deltamethrin and chlorotoluron as substances of concern whose approvals have been more than doubled by extensions, and asks the Commission to stop yearly extensions.
- Why it matters: It ties deltamethrin to a legal argument that NGOs have already won in court on another substance, so further deltamethrin extensions are open to litigation (for example an internal review request). The PPP route is indirect, but the stated concern rests on hazard (DNT). It may also prompt scrutiny of the BPR expiry postponement for deltamethrin.
- NGO: PAN Europe. [Letter to C. Bury, "Lessons from recent EU verdicts on pesticides" (PDF)](https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/20260513%20Letter%20to%20C.%20Bury_Lessons%20from%20recent%20EU%20verdicts%20on%20pesticides%201.pdf)

**2026-04-30 — PAN Europe asks EU member states to vote against a tenth extension of deltamethrin's farm-pesticide approval to June 2028, citing evidence that it harms children's brain development**
`PPP` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: In its position letter for the 5–6 May 2026 SCoPAFF meeting, PAN Europe says deltamethrin's initial approval expired in October 2013 and that the proposal "would extend it until June 2028, nearly fifteen years beyond the original approval period", which is "particularly alarming given that evidence of developmental neurotoxicity has been identified". It asks members to oppose the extension. An earlier PAN Europe blog (2026-03-10) made the same point about the March proposal.
- Why it matters: This keeps the DNT hazard case in front of member-state experts, many of whom also sit in biocide competent authorities. The route is indirect (PPP), but the argument is hazard-based and carries over strongly.
- NGO: PAN Europe. [SCoPAFF letter 30 April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.pan-europe.info/sites/pan-europe.info/files/public/resources/Letters/Letter_30042026_SCoPAFF%20letter%205%20May.pdf)

**2026-04-24 — PAN Germany asks Bayer at its AGM to withdraw deltamethrin worldwide, citing evidence linking it to Parkinson's disease**
`PPP` · `international` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: In a speech at Bayer's AGM, PAN Germany (Dr Peter Clausing) asked Bayer to withdraw fipronil, deltamethrin and mancozeb globally because of evidence linking them to Parkinson's disease, and called for Parkinson-relevant neurotoxicity testing in approval procedures.
- Why it matters: It adds a second neurotoxicity endpoint (neurodegeneration) to the hazard case and targets a major registrant directly. The route is indirect, but hazard-based.
- NGO: PAN Germany. [Redebeitrag auf der Bayer-Hauptversammlung am 24.04.2026](https://pan-germany.org/pestizide/redebeitrag-auf-der-bayer-hauptversammlung-am-24-04-2026/)

**2026-04-13 — Deutsche Umwelthilfe sues Germany's pesticide authority to revoke two deltamethrin insecticides, citing nerve damage at very low doses and risks to unborn children's brains**
`PPP` · `national:DE` · `indirect–hazard` · `litigation`
- Summary: DUH filed suit against the BVL to revoke the authorisations of Decis Forte and GAT DECLINE 2.5 EC. It cites new evidence of neurotoxic and hormonal interactions with the developing nervous system at doses below the EFSA NOAEL, and risk to pregnant women via food residues. DUH says it will go "bis zur letzten Instanz" (to the court of last instance).
- Why it matters: This is the first NGO litigation found on deltamethrin. A court finding that the product risk assessment ignores newer DNT evidence would strengthen the hazard case across regimes. It is indirect (PPP products), but hazard-based, so it carries over strongly.
- NGO: Deutsche Umwelthilfe. [Nervengift im Ackerbau: Deutsche Umwelthilfe klagt gegen zwei gesundheits- und umweltschädliche Pestizide](https://www.duh.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung/nervengift-im-ackerbau-deutsche-umwelthilfe-klagt-gegen-zwei-gesundheits-und-umweltschaedliche-pest/)

**2025-11-20 — PAN Germany calls for pyrethroids including deltamethrin to become EU priority water pollutants and to be regulated in all uses, including biocides and veterinary medicines, citing extreme toxicity to aquatic life**
`water` · `EU` · `indirect–hazard` · `study/report`
- Summary: The article reviews science and regulation on pyrethroids. It notes that deltamethrin is one of four pyrethroids proposed as priority substances under the Water Framework Directive, and asks for group environmental quality standards, better monitoring, public sales and use data, and regulation of pyrethroids "in all application areas including biocides and veterinary medicines".
- Why it matters: Priority-substance status and tighter environmental quality standards would feed into the BPR environmental risk assessment (PNECs, STP and surface-water emissions from PT18 uses). It rests on intrinsic aquatic toxicity, so it carries over strongly. It asks for regulation across regimes but is not a request on the BPR approval itself.
- NGO: PAN Germany. [Extrem toxisch für Wasserorganismen: Pyrethroid-Insektizide im Fokus von Wissenschaft und Regulation](https://pan-germany.org/pestizide/extrem-toxisch-fuer-wasserorganismen-pyrethroid-insektizide-im-fokus-von-wissenschaft-und-regulation/)

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

- **DUH v BVL (Germany):** the case is pending. DUH says it will go to the court of last instance.
- **EU PPP extension and renewal:** PAN Europe opposed the extension to June 2028 and, in its 2026-05-13 letter, argued that such repeated extensions conflict with the General Court's T-412/22 ruling. This leaves room for an internal review request or litigation against the extension regulation. WWF Switzerland's factsheet says the EU review is expected to finish in 2026. PAN Europe can be expected to push its DNT dossier into the EFSA and SCoPAFF renewal stage.
- **DNT data requirements:** PAN Europe and PAN Germany want mandatory developmental neurotoxicity and Parkinson-relevant testing. If adopted horizontally, these would raise the bar for every regime, including BPR.
- **Biocides Omnibus X:** Générations Futures is lobbying DG SANTE and DG ENV against unlimited biocide approvals. The negotiations are ongoing.
- **France:** per Générations Futures, ANSES is preparing phytopharmacovigilance sheets on deltamethrin and wants to separate agricultural and biocidal contributions to pyrethroid exposure. Vector-control spraying, such as during the chikungunya outbreak, is drawing local opposition.
- **Water:** PAN Germany is pushing pyrethroid priority-substance status under the WFD. In Switzerland, the Federal Council's decision on the water ordinance is pending.
