# NGO report: IR3535 (ethyl butylacetylaminopropionate), CAS 52304-36-6

## 1. Summary

Both top signals target biocidal repellent products (BPR) directly. Neither targets another regime, and no NGO has gone after the active substance approval itself. The only NGO activity on IR3535 in the window comes from consumer organisations testing mosquito repellents in summer 2025 and 2026. No environmental, health or legal NGO (PAN, ClientEarth, HEAL, Générations Futures, GLOBAL 2000 and others) published anything on it. The most relevant item for the renewal is from Altroconsumo, Italy's consumer association. It now advises against IR3535 products for children and pregnant women "as a precaution" until the endocrine-disruptor evaluation, opened in 2024, is finished. It also rates IR3535 as weakly effective. Test-Aankoop/Test-Achats in Belgium raises only efficacy problems: IR3535 products failed its 2026 test. Its French-language buying guide still calls IR3535 safe for children over 6 months and for pregnant women. Overall pressure is low, but there is now an ED-based "avoid" message in the public eye. It would grow if the BPR ED assessment comes back positive.

## 2. Top signals

### Tier A: direct

**2026-08-07 — Italian consumer group Altroconsumo tells parents to avoid IR3535 repellents for children, citing the open EU check on whether it disrupts hormones and its weak protection against mosquitoes**
`BPR` · `national:IT` · `direct` · `study/report`
- Summary: Altroconsumo's 2026 repellent test describes IR3535 as "less effective, rarely distributed and under evaluation as an endocrine disruptor", and says it should be avoided as a precaution for children. Its standing buying guide (undated, current in 2026) goes further. It says IR3535 "nel 2024 è stato incluso nella lista delle sostanze da valutare per i possibili effetti interferenti endocrini" and that, given valid alternatives exist, "lo sconsigliamo per i bambini e le donne in gravidanza, finché non sarà conclusa la valutazione". In the test, IR3535 products got medium or low scores.
- Why it matters: This targets IR3535 biocidal products directly and rests on hazard grounds (the pending ED assessment), which carry over strongly. It is consumer advice, not a formal request, so it puts no procedural pressure on the Belgian CA's renewal evaluation. Its weight is that it links IR3535 to "endocrine disruptor" in public, and it pairs the hazard concern with an efficacy and substitution argument ("valid alternatives exist"). That is relevant to the open candidate-for-substitution question. If the BPR ED assessment confirms ED properties, this framing could easily be picked up by other Euroconsumers members and by health NGOs.
- NGO: Altroconsumo, [2026 test news](https://www.altroconsumo.it/salute/repellenti-zanzare/news/test-repellenti-antizanzare); [buying guide with the ED advice](https://www.altroconsumo.it/salute/repellenti-zanzare/come-scegliere)

**2026-06-15 — Belgian consumer group Test-Aankoop/Test-Achats warns that its three failing mosquito products all contain IR3535, citing weaker protection than DEET, icaridin or eucalyptus citriodora oil**
`BPR` · `national:BE` · `direct` · `study/report`
- Summary: Test-Aankoop tested 23 repellents. Three gave "insufficient protection" against mosquitoes, and all three contain IR3535: Kruidvat Anti-insect sensitive spray, Mouskito Kids North Europe lotion and Mouskito North Europe vapo. The test calls IR3535 "less effective against mosquitoes than other active ingredients". The article was updated on 2026-08-07 with the same findings. No safety concern or demand to authorities is raised, and the separate French-language buying guide calls IR3535 safe for adults, children over 6 months and pregnant women.
- Why it matters: This targets biocidal products directly, but on efficacy, not hazard. Efficacy findings mostly affect product authorisations, not the substance renewal. They could support a substitution argument if IR3535 is flagged as a candidate for substitution. Impact on the renewal is weak. Belgium is the evaluating CA, so national visibility is slightly more relevant than usual.
- NGO: Test-Aankoop/Test-Achats, [2026 test (NL)](https://www.test-aankoop.be/gezond/dagelijkse-verzorging/antimuggenmiddelen/nieuws/het-beste-middel-tegen-muggen); [2026 update](https://www.test-aankoop.be/gezond/dagelijkse-verzorging/antimuggenmiddelen/nieuws/beste-muggenspray-2026)

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

- Altroconsumo says it advises against IR3535 for children and pregnant women only "until the evaluation is concluded". It says it may revise its position after the ED assessment outcome. A positive ED finding would likely harden this into a general "avoid" message, and other Euroconsumers members (Test-Aankoop/Test-Achats, OCU, DECO Proteste) might follow.
- Consumer repellent tests come out every summer (June to August). The next round is expected in summer 2027, before the 2028-04-30 approval expiry.
- No NGO has announced any campaign, litigation or formal request on IR3535.
