## 1. Summary

No NGO activity specific to hydrogen cyanide (HCN) as a biocide or pesticide was found in the window 2024-10-05 to 2026-10-05, and none in the focus period from 2026-04-05. None of the NGOs named in the brief, and none of the others checked, has published a campaign, report, letter, legal filing or testing result targeting HCN fumigants (e.g. URAGAN D2). Where HCN appears on NGO sites, it is incidental: a combustion by-product of plastics or fire smoke, or a natural compound in plants. The overall direction is neutral. HCN sits outside current NGO pesticide and biocide campaigns, which focus on agricultural PPPs, neonicotinoids, PFAS pesticides, endocrine disruptors and exports of banned pesticides.
