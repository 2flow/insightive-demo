## 1. Summary

None of the top signals target the biocide approval, and no NGO activity on sodium cacodylate (sodium dimethylarsinate / cacodylic acid) in any regime was found for 2024-10-06 to 2026-10-06. There is no campaign, no litigation, no formal request and no NGO testing that names the substance. Its only NGO footprint is as a standing entry in PAN's hazard lists (the PAN International Highly Hazardous Pesticides list and an older PAN Germany list of biocides that may meet cut-off criteria). These are reference lists, not targeted actions, and the entry in the current edition could not be confirmed. The direction is neutral. NGOs pay no attention to this obsolete arsenical herbicide, which is still under initial PT18 evaluation (Portugal). That could change if the evaluation leads to a public consultation, for example on a candidate-for-substitution designation, because arsenic compounds are easy targets for campaigns.
