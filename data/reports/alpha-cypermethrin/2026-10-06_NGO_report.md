## 1. Summary

Of the 2 top signals, 1 targets the PPP regime and 1 is cross-regime (a PPP export-ban case that also asks for biocides to be covered). None targets the EU biocide approval directly. The only real NGO activity on alpha-cypermethrin in the window is French. In June 2025 activists found BASF's Fastac (alpha-cypermethrin) at the company's Genay plant near Lyon. Générations Futures then asked why the plant still held a product banned since 2020, and on 2026-02-09 it filed two complaints with the Lyon judicial court, together with Alerte Planète and backed by Extinction Rebellion. Their demands include extending France's export ban to "substances et produits biocides" that share an active substance with a banned pesticide. That demand points straight at the gap that keeps alpha-cypermethrin legal (approved as a PT18 biocide but withdrawn as a PPP). Outside France, EU-level NGOs (PAN Europe, ClientEarth, GLOBAL 2000) are focused on cypermethrin, not alpha-cypermethrin. Overall direction: low pressure, but the export and "same substance, two regimes" angle could grow.

### Tier B: indirect, hazard-based

**2026-02-09 — French NGOs take BASF to court over banned alpha-cypermethrin stocks made for export, and ask for the export ban to cover biocides with the same active substance, citing its neurotoxicity and harm to aquatic life and pollinators**
`cross-regime` · `national:FR` · `indirect–hazard` · `litigation`
- Summary: Générations Futures and Alerte Planète, with support from Extinction Rebellion, filed two complaints with the Lyon judicial court. The complaints concern BASF holding and selling, at its Genay site, pesticide products whose active substances are banned in the EU, chiefly Fastac, which "est produit à base d'alpha-cyperméthrine". The NGOs say tens of thousands of litres were meant for export outside the EU, and that production, storage and circulation have been prohibited under French law since 2022 (and for the active substances themselves since 2026-01-01). They also demand "l'extension de cette interdiction aux substances et produits biocides pour lesquelles la substance active est identique à celle d'un pesticide interdit", and want France to push for these measures at EU level.
- Why it matters for the BPR renewal: This is indirect. It targets French export law and the PPP ban, not the EU biocide approval. But it is the only item found that names the biocide use of alpha-cypermethrin as a loophole. It rests on hazard grounds (a substance banned as a PPP), which carry over strongly. If the "same active substance" argument spreads, it adds political pressure on the PT18 renewal and the candidate-for-substitution assessment. In public reporting BASF says the Genay alpha-cypermethrin is legal because biocides fall outside the French law.
- NGO: Générations Futures (with Alerte Planète, Extinction Rebellion): https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/basf-fastac-pesticides/

**2025-06-27 — Générations Futures demands enforcement after banned alpha-cypermethrin insecticide is found at a BASF plant, citing neurotoxicity and harm to aquatic life and pollinators**
`PPP` · `national:FR` · `indirect–hazard` · `statement`
- Summary: After "faucheurs volontaires" activists entered the BASF Genay site on 2025-06-23, Générations Futures reported that Fastac, a product based on alpha-cypermethrin and banned in France since 2020, was still on site. It noted that the authorities had known since February 2024. It describes alpha-cypermethrin as neurotoxic and very dangerous to aquatic ecosystems and pollinating insects, said it was considering legal follow-up, and demanded that the law be enforced.
- Why it matters for the BPR renewal: This is indirect. It concerns the PPP ban and French export law. It is hazard-based, so it reinforces a hazard picture (neurotoxic, aquatic and pollinator toxicity) that carries over to the BPR file, and it led to the February 2026 complaint above.
- NGO: Générations Futures: https://www.generations-futures.fr/actualites/pesticide-interdit-fastac/

## 5. What NGOs say is coming next

- The Lyon judicial court complaints against BASF (filed 2026-02-09) are pending. No hearing date is published on the Générations Futures page.
- Générations Futures and partners want France to argue at EU level for a production and export ban on all prohibited pesticides, whatever the reason for withdrawal, extended to biocides with the same active substance. This would apply directly to alpha-cypermethrin (approved for PT18, not for PPP).
- Générations Futures' broader call to withdraw all pyrethroid-based products, biocides included (2025-04), remains open.
