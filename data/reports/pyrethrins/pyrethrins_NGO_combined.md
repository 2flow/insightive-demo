**2025-04-07: PAN Netherlands wants pyrethrin taken off the shelves for home and garden use and asks retailers and the government to phase out consumer insecticide products, citing harm to bees and other insects, aquatic toxicity and possible endocrine effects**
`cross-regime` · `national:NL` · `direct` · `campaign`

- **Summary:** PAN-NL looked at the pesticides that private users can buy online and at Dutch garden centres (Welkoop, Praxis, Intratuin, bol.com and others). It published a "black list" of 10 insecticides it says are too harmful for non-professional use: deltamethrin, flupyradifurone, imidacloprid, lambda-cyhalothrin, permethrin, prallethrin, pyrethrin, spinosad, tetramethrin and transfluthrin. Four pyrethrin products are named. Two are plant-protection sprays (ECOstyle Promanal, Pokon Bio Polysect). Two are home pest-control products of the biocide type: Solabiol Natria fly and mosquito spray (pyrethrins 0.2% + PBO 1%) and ECOstyle ant powder based on "natuur-pyrethrum". The report says pyrethrin is not persistent but is "zeer toxisch voor aquatische en land insecten" (very toxic to aquatic and land insects), may be endocrine-disrupting, and may affect reproduction. It also flags PBO, which is often co-formulated. PAN-NL asks retailers and webshops to stop selling products with harmful substances to private users by 2027 and to adopt the black list. It asks the government to regulate toward a ban on chemical pesticides for non-professional use.
- **Why it matters for the BPR renewal:** This is the only NGO item in the window that names pyrethrins-based biocidal products directly. The route is direct, but the effect is national and falls on products. It targets retail availability and amateur-use authorisations in the Netherlands, not the EU active-substance approval, which runs to 2034/2035. The arguments mix hazard (insect and aquatic toxicity, suspected ED) and use (exposure from untrained private users). The hazard points carry over to other regimes, but they are cited from the PPDB rather than from new evidence, so their weight is limited. Short-term risk is reputational and retail delisting of consumer PT18 products in NL, plus possible pressure on Ctgb amateur-use authorisations. The "suspected ED" claim is worth watching if it is picked up elsewhere.
- NGO: and link:** PAN Netherlands, "PAN-NL zet 10 insecticiden op de zwarte lijst" (research report, 7 April 2025), [Onderzoeksrapport (PDF)](https://www.pan-netherlands.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/onderzoeksrapport.pdf)
